One platform.
Every department.

Fennec Dash is a self-hosted business platform: CRM, ERP, CMS, MAP and PIM in one system, with 21 modules sharing one database and one login.

CRM • ERP • CMS • MAP • PIM

Your data, your servers.
One purchase, no subscription.

Self-hosted, no cloud lock-in, no third-party data collection. Privacy is architecture, not a setting.

Law 18-07 compliance, DZD pricing, Arabic and French interfaces, local payment gateways.

Support comes from the team that writes the code — not a reseller reading a script.

Operations-heavy companies of 5–250 people in Algeria and MENA: trade and distribution, manufacturing, logistics, real estate and professional services. The buyer is an owner or operations director who has outgrown spreadsheets and does not want a foreign cloud subscription.

Boilerplate — 50 words

Fennec Dash is a self-hosted business platform with 21 integrated modules covering CRM, ERP, HR, finance, logistics and marketing. Built on PHP 8 and MySQL, it is designed for Algerian compliance, priced in DZD, and available in English, French and Arabic.

Give it room. Keep it small.

The logo works because of the space around it. Clear space is proportional, not decorative — it survives every size change.
x x x x
x = ½ of the logo height. At the 74 mm width shown, x = 12 mm. Minimum clear space is 4 mm at any size.

Purple — no#6B21A8
The lockup has no white-ink variant. On purple, use the fennec mark alone in gold or place the logo outside the colour field.
Placement

Default position is top-left, aligned to the page or container margin. In the marketing navbar it sits left of the navigation; in the admin it sits at the top of the 230 px sidebar. Never centre it in a navigation bar, and never place it inside a bordered box that is not part of the layout.

Photography

Only the reversed lockup, over a flat scrim of at least 60% black. Test that the gold mark still reads at thumbnail size before publishing. If the image is busy behind the wordmark, move the logo — do not add a glow or a second scrim.

L-01

MustMaintain x = ½ logo height on all four sides. Nothing enters that zone — text, rules, edges, or other logos.

L-02

NeverPlace the primary lockup on #6B21A8 or any mid-tone surface. Contrast is insufficient and no approved variant exists.

Eight ways to break it.

Every item below has shipped at least once. None of them is a style preference — each one damages legibility, ownership or both.
×Stretch or squashWidth and height are linked by the 3.0963:1 ratio.
×RecolourNo black, white, gold-only or gradient versions exist.
×Rotate or skewOnly 0° is approved, on every surface.
×Add shadow, glow or outlineDepth comes from layout, never from the logo.
×Use on brand purpleThe ink wordmark disappears; no reverse variant exists.
Fennec Dash
live text, wrong typeface
×Rebuild in typeThe wordmark is custom artwork, not Plus Jakarta Sans.
×Crop or separateNever crop the mark, or split it from the wordmark.
×Fade or watermarkIf it needs to recede, remove it instead.
Also forbidden
Adding taglines, URLs, certification badges or “since 2019” inside the lockup. Combining the logo with another company’s mark to imply partnership without written approval. Animating the logo in product UI.
Favicon exception
The current favicon uses a legacy gold (#FFC834) and is only 29 × 35 px. It is the one approved use of that colour, and it should be re-exported from the mark at 512 px with logo gold #FAC259 (action A-02).
If you see a broken logo
Do not “fix” it by recreating the artwork. Replace it with the supplied file at the correct proportion, or remove it and flag the asset to Product & Brand.

Two dark palettes
The product uses #121212 / #1A1A1A / #2D2D2D. The marketing site uses #0F0F12 / #1E1E2D. They are not interchangeable: the marketing palette is warmer and deeper for full-bleed editorial sections; the product palette is neutral for long working sessions.
Token discipline
Components consume tokens, never raw hex. If a component needs a colour that does not exist, add a token in fennec.css, document it here, then use it. Three competing theme files is how the current drift started.
Known contrast defect
White text on the dark-mode primary #8B5CF6 measures 4.23:1 — below AA for 13.5 px button labels. Use #7C3AED as the fill for text buttons in dark mode (5.70:1), and keep #8B5CF6 for icons, borders and large type. Fix tracked as A-03.

Why not one family?
Plus Jakarta Sans is a display face: its wide apertures and tight default spacing break down in 12 px table headers and dense forms. Inter was designed for interfaces, has true tabular numerals and a taller x-height. Using either for both jobs produces the exact inconsistency this guide exists to stop.

ISO codes (DZD, EUR, USD) belong in data, settings and API payloads. Symbols and suffixes belong on screen.

Measure & rhythm

Body copy runs 60–75 characters per line. On a 1440 px canvas that means a maximum text width of about 720 px; never let a paragraph fill a full-width panel. Paragraph spacing is one line (20 px in product, 28 px on marketing), not an empty paragraph tag.

Alignment. Left-align everything by default, including headings. Centre only hero copy, empty states and short quotes of two lines or fewer. Never justify.

Multilingual setting
English

Source language. en-US spelling. Oxford comma. Sentence case in product.

Français

Allow +30% string length. Keep the space before : ; ! ?. Do not remove accents from capitals.

العربية

True RTL. Never force Inter on Arabic glyphs; use the system Arabic stack. Add 25% line height.

Arabic type stack
"Segoe UI", Tahoma, "Noto Naskh Arabic", "Arial", sans-serif — set dir="rtl" and lang="ar" on the document, not just on a wrapper. Latin brand names inside Arabic copy keep their own direction and font.
T-04

NeverUse synthetic bold or italic. Load the weight; do not let the browser fake it.

T-05

NeverPut essential copy inside an image or a screenshot. It cannot be translated, selected or read aloud.

T-06

MustTest every string with a 30% length increase and in RTL before release. French and Arabic are shipped languages, not future options.

Borders

Hairlines are 1 px, in --border, never black and never a brand colour. Focus is the only 2 px border. A coloured left border is reserved for status callouts (3 px) and active navigation (3 px).

Dark mode

Shadows get deeper, not lighter: card shadow alpha moves from 8% to 30%, dropdown from 12% to 40%. Elevation is perceived through contrast with the surface, and dark surfaces absorb light.

E-01

MustUse one elevation per surface. A card with a border does not also need a heavy shadow; a dropdown does not get a border and a 25 px glow.

E-02

NeverApply shadow to table rows, input fields or text. Elevation describes containers only.

08 — Motion Behaviour
08

Fast, then still.

Motion confirms a change and then gets out of the way. If an animation can be removed without losing information, remove it.
Durations
Micro — hover, focus, toggle150 ms
Standard — dropdown, tooltip200 ms
Surface — modal, drawer300 ms
Expand — accordion, panel400 ms
Entrance — hero, first paint800 ms
Ambient — decorative only6 s loop
Standardcubic-bezier(.4, 0, .2, 1)
Entranceease-out
What may animate

Transform and opacity only. Width, height, top, left, margin and box-shadow trigger layout or paint and drop frames on large tables. If a height change is unavoidable, animate max-height once and stop.

One loop per view. A pulsing “Popular” badge, a floating decorative shape, a shimmer — pick one. Two loops compete for attention and neither wins.

What never animates

Table rows, chart data on every poll, the logo, form validation errors, or anything that moves while the user is typing. Product screens should be still by default; motion belongs to marketing.

Reduced motion
@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) sets animation and transition duration to 0.01ms. This is already implemented in landing.css; it is mandatory in every new stylesheet.

Exit is always faster than entrance — 150 ms, no stagger. One pattern per view; never combine a fade, a slide and a scale on the same element.
M-01

MustUse 150 ms for control feedback and 300 ms for surfaces. Anything above 400 ms in the product feels broken, not smooth.

M-03

NeverUse motion to conceal loading. A skeleton that fades is fine; a spinner that hides a 4 s query is a performance bug.

Confident. Concrete. Local.

We sound like the engineer who built the product and the operator who uses it. No hype, no borrowed Silicon Valley vocabulary, no adjectives doing the work of facts.

Release checklist — UI & copy
  • Logo is the supplied file, correct variant, ratio intact
  • Clear space ≥ ½ logo height on all sides
  • Only guide hex values; no legacy colour introduced
  • Text contrast ≥ 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text
  • Focus visible on every interactive element
  • One primary button per view or card
  • Money right-aligned, tabular, one currency per table
  • Type roles from the scale; no new sizes
  • Spacing from the 4/8 px scale
  • One icon family; icon-only buttons labelled
  • French and Arabic strings exist; RTL verified
